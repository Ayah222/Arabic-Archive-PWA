---
name: Notification audiences
description: Audience boundary for administrative and employee notifications
---

Administrative alerts—especially pending employee approval requests—must be visible only to managers. Employees must not see these alerts or have them included in their unread counts.

**Why:** Approval workflows and user management are manager-only responsibilities. Employee-specific notifications are a separate product flow to be defined later.

**How to apply:** Mark manager-only notifications with the admin audience and filter them from every employee notification surface, including the bell count and the notifications page.
